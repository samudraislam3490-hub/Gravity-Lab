How to Play

1. Control Gravity

Press SPACE or tap the ⟳ Reverse button (mobile) to reverse gravity. Your ball will fall upward or downward depending on the current direction. Watch the gravity indicator arrow!

2. Move & Jump

Desktop: Use ← → arrow keys or A D to move. Press ↑ or W to jump.
Mobile: Tap left/right sides of the screen to move. Tap the screen center to jump.

3. Collect Crystals

Grab the glowing blue energy crystals for +10 points each. They respawn after collection.

4. Avoid Obstacles

Red spikes, blocks, and lasers will destroy you on contact. They get faster and more frequent over time!

5. Survive & Score

Score increases +1 per second survived. Beat your high score! A random gravity fact appears after each game.
