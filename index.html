How to Play

1. Control Gravity

Press SPACE or tap the ⟳ Reverse button (mobile) to reverse gravity. Gravity also changes automatically during gameplay!

2. Move & Jump

Desktop: Use ← → or A D to move. Press ↑ or W to jump.
Mobile: Tap left/right sides of the screen to move. Tap center to jump.

3. Collect Crystals

Grab glowing energy crystals for +10 points each.

4. Avoid Obstacles

Red spikes, blocks, and lasers destroy you. Watch for laser warnings!

5. Survive & Score

Score +1/second survived. Beat your high score!
